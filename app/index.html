DevX PayPerCall

Escrowed USDC pay-per-call for AI agents. Pay $0.01, get a verified answer or an automatic refund, on Arc.

Status: in development. The contract and app are being built in Arc Studio and tested on Arc testnet. This page will become the live app.

1 · Escrow

An agent locks the exact price of one call in USDC with requestCall, or gaslessly with an EIP-3009 signature.

2 · Verdict

The provider's bot answers off-chain and settles onchain with keccak256 of the exact response, so the answer stays provable.

3 · Split or refund

Payment splits provider / referrer (90 / 10). If the call is rejected or the deadline passes, the agent gets a full refund.
